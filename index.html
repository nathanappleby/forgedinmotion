The Problem

It was never really
about the pain.

You didn't come to physical therapy because your shoulder hurts. You came because you can't bench press. Because you can't get through a run without it flaring up. Because you had to sit out of the game with your kid instead of throwing the ball around.

Pain is just the symptom. What it's actually costing you is the thing you love doing. That's the problem worth solving — and most treatment plans never even address it.

Pain often creates more uncertainty and fear than it does actual physical limitation. Once that fear is gone, most people are far more capable than they think.

"Nobody comes to me because they want to 'get out of pain.' They come because they want back to their sport, their gym, their kids. I build every plan around that — not around the injury in isolation."

About Nathan
